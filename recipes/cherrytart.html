Cherry Tart

Description

With a creamy vanilla custard filling, fresh cherries, and a crust that doesn't require refrigeration or rolling before using, this cherry tart is the best. A dessert that comes together quickly and looks sophisticated.

Ingredients

For the crust

  • Unsalted butter
  • Granulated sugar
  • Flour
  • Salt
  • Ground almonds

For the filling

  • Cherries
  • Vanilla
  • Cream
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Flour

Steps

  1. Prepare the Pie Crust:
    • In a large mixing bowl, combine 2 ½ cups of all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon of salt, and 1 tablespoon of sugar. Mix well.
    • Add 1 cup of cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes, to the flour mixture. Use a pastry cutter or your fingers to cut the butter into the flour until it resembles coarse crumbs.
    • Slowly add 6-8 tablespoons of ice water, one tablespoon at a time, mixing with a fork until the dough comes together.
    • Divide the dough in half and shape each half into a disk. Wrap them in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  2. Prepare the Cherry Filling:
    • In a large bowl, combine 4 cups of pitted cherries, 1 cup of granulated sugar, ¼ cup of cornstarch, 1 tablespoon of lemon juice, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract. Mix well to coat the cherries evenly.
  3. Roll out the Pie Crust:
    • Take one of the chilled dough disks and place it on a lightly floured surface. Roll it out into a circle, about 12 inches in diameter, and about ¼ inch thick. Carefully transfer the rolled-out crust to a 9-inch pie dish.
  4. Fill the Pie:
    • Pour the prepared cherry filling into the pie crust, spreading it evenly.
  5. Add the Top Crust:
    • Roll out the second dough disk into a circle of the same size as the first one. You can either place the entire crust on top of the filling, cutting slits for venting, or create a lattice pattern by cutting strips of dough and weaving them over the filling.
  6. Seal and Decorate the Edges:
    • Trim any excess dough hanging over the edges of the pie dish. Press the edges of the top and bottom crusts together to seal. Flute the edges with your fingers or use a fork to create a decorative pattern.
  7. Bake the Pie:
    • Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
    • Place the cherry pie on a baking sheet to catch any drips. Bake for 15 minutes at 425°F, then reduce the oven temperature to 375°F (190°C) and continue baking for another 35-45 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
